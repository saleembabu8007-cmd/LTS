# LTSGROUP Corporate Website Redesign — Master Rules & Guidelines

You are redesigning the complete LTSGROUP corporate website.

This is not a generic corporate website redesign.

You must approach this project as a senior digital product designer, UX architect, art director, information architect, and frontend design-system engineer working together.

The final website must look sufficiently polished and intentional that a real corporate client can review the completed design and confidently approve it for production.

---

## 1. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Create a premium, modern, engineering-led digital presence for LTSGROUP.

The website must communicate:
- Engineering expertise
- Technical capability
- Operational scale
- Reliability
- Professionalism
- Structured business divisions
- Project capability
- Long-term corporate credibility
- Modern thinking
- Breadth of services

### Core Aesthetic Balance
- The design must feel **established** without looking old-fashioned.
- It must feel **modern** without looking like an AI-generated startup website.
- It must feel **premium** without becoming an architecture portfolio.
- It must feel **technical** without becoming visually cold or overly industrial.

## 5. DESIGN GOAL & SYSTEM RULES

**Think:**
"Contemporary engineering group website."

**Not:**
- "AI-generated construction website."
- "Generic consulting website."
- "Template website."
- "Portfolio website."

### Architectural Requirements
1. **Purposeful Sections:** Every section must have a definitive reason to exist.
2. **Hierarchy First:** Every component must support the information hierarchy.
3. **Unified Design System:** Every page must feel like part of one cohesive, scalable design system.
4. **Foundation Before Detail:** Before implementing detailed pages, establish the complete design system foundations (tokens, typography, colors, layout components) and robust information architecture.
